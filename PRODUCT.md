# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and tech leads evaluating Krzysztof Gryc for a Senior React Developer role (employment or contract). They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a job application, usually on a laptop, sometimes on a phone, and have minutes, not hours. Their job: judge seniority, stack fit, track record and how others rate working with him, then download the CV or get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio / contact site at krzysztofg.pl. Success: a recruiter leaves with a clear picture of who he is and what he works in, and acts (downloads the CV, writes an e-mail, calls, opens LinkedIn).

## Positioning

A senior React developer who came from graphic design and DTP (pre-2014) and freelance web design, so he builds front ends with a designer's eye. Ten-plus years in web; current work centres on a large editor in a media company's back-office system. Five signed recommendations from employers and colleagues, with references available on request.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js (pages router) with styled-components; styled-components must stay.

- Separate URLs (/resume, /recommendation, /contact) were not marked as required.
- CV PDFs: `public/utils/krzysztofgryc_pl.pdf`, `public/utils/krzysztofgryc_en.pdf`.

## Product Principles

1. Recruiter speed first: role, stack and contact visible without hunting.
2. Proof over adjectives: real recommendations and real career facts carry the claim.
3. The site itself is a work sample: its front-end craft is evidence of the skill being hired.
4. Bilingual parity: PL and EN carry the same content and weight.
